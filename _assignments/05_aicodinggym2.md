## Overview

This is your second AI Coding Gym challenge. Building on the experience from Challenge #1, you will tackle a harder problem -- a **Silver-tier** challenge where the AI makes systematic errors (e.g., wrong cross-validation strategy, failure to handle class imbalance, data leakage). You must diagnose the error pattern and redesign the pipeline.

The goal remains the same: **outperform the AI baseline** on the [AI Coding Gym](https://aicodinggym.com/) platform, but this time you will need deeper diagnostic skills and more strategic intervention.


## Learning Loop

Same five-step loop as Challenge #1:

1. **Inspect the AI agent's work.** This time, pay special attention to *systematic* errors in the AI's pipeline -- not just suboptimal choices but fundamentally flawed methodology.

2. **Attempt the challenge using AI as a tool.** Redesign the pipeline where the AI went wrong. Direct your AI assistant with specific corrections rather than accepting its defaults.

3. **Score comparison.** Submit predictions on the AI Coding Gym platform.

4. **Work-in-progress sharing (in-class).** We will again dedicate class time for peer presentations. Focus on *what you diagnosed* in the AI's pipeline and how you fixed it.

5. **Reflection write-up.** Write a structured reflection (see Deliverables below).


## Deliverables

### 1. Challenge Submission (via AI Coding Gym)
- Submit your predictions on the [AI Coding Gym](https://aicodinggym.com/) platform.
- You may submit as many times as you like. Your best score will be recorded.

### 2. Final Report (via Canvas)
Submit a **2-3 page report** (PDF) on Canvas containing:

- **AI Inspection Summary**: What systematic errors did the AI agent make? How did you trace them through the agent's reasoning and code?

- **Diagnosis and Fix**: What was fundamentally wrong with the AI's approach? How did you redesign the pipeline? What domain knowledge or data science judgment was required that the AI lacked?

- **Strategy Evolution**: How did your approach to working with AI change from Challenge #1 to Challenge #2? What did you learn about directing AI tools more effectively?

- **Results Comparison**: Your score vs. the AI baseline. Include a screenshot of your leaderboard position.

- **Reflection** answering three questions:
  1. Where did the AI fall short?
  2. What did I do that the AI did not?
  3. What concept or skill did this teach me?


## Grading

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Challenge score (from AI Coding Gym leaderboard) | 30% |
| Reflection write-up (depth of analysis, not just reporting scores) | 40% |

**Note:** Since this is a Silver-tier challenge, the AI's errors are more subtle. Strong grades require demonstrating that you understand *why* the AI failed, not just that you achieved a better score.
